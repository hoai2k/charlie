# American Girl Doll Race: music requests

Songs for the race tracks that don't have their own yet. Every prompt builds on
the game's house sound, the base prompt below, and folds in that track's
character. Generate with Suno (or similar), then drop the MP3 into
`intake/` named after the song. It will be loudness-matched and set as the
track's `music` in `tracks.js` (see `intake/README.md`).

## Base prompt (the house sound)

> K-pop dance-pop with punchy kick-snare and sparkling synth plucks over a
> bouncy four-on-the-floor groove; verse stays tight and playful with
> staccato delivery, pre-chorus opens into stacked harmonies and a rising
> filter sweep, chorus bursts with group vocals and a bright synth hook,
> bridge drops to clipped handclaps and breathy doubles before the final
> chorus lifts bigger. Crisp, glossy mix with cute ear-candy chimes, reverse
> swells, and short delay throws on the hook word.

Aim for 2 to 3 minutes. A song plays on repeat through a race, so a clean
ending (no long fade-out) loops best.

## Open requests

### M2. Candy Land (`candy`)

> Sugary K-pop dance-pop with punchy kick-snare and sparkling candy-bright
> synth plucks over a bouncy four-on-the-floor groove with a rubbery
> gumdrop bassline; verse stays tight and playful with staccato delivery and
> fizzing pop-rock percussion, pre-chorus opens into stacked
> cotton-candy harmonies and a rising filter sweep, chorus bursts with group
> vocals and a bright music-box synth hook, bridge drops to clipped
> handclaps, bubble pops and breathy doubles before the final chorus lifts
> bigger. Crisp, glossy mix with cute ear-candy chimes, wrapper-crinkle
> reverse swells, and short delay throws on the hook word.

### M4. Cloud Kingdom (`clouds`)

> Dreamy K-pop dance-pop with punchy kick-snare and sparkling synth plucks
> over a bouncy four-on-the-floor groove under soft, airy pads; verse stays
> tight and playful with staccato delivery and floating harp arpeggios,
> pre-chorus opens into stacked harmonies and a rising filter sweep like
> lifting off, chorus bursts with group vocals and a bright rainbow synth
> hook, bridge drops to a floaty half-time of clipped handclaps, wind
> chimes and breathy doubles before the final chorus lifts bigger. Crisp,
> glossy mix with cute ear-candy chimes, whooshing reverse swells, and short
> delay throws on the hook word.
