    Drag a landmark handle to move it (frame pixels, whole numbers). Drag an item to offset it, its round handle to rotate and its square handle to scale. Drag the background (or two fingers) to pan; wheel or pinch to zoom. Edits autosave in this browser; Download edits makes the file to hand over (see party/workbench/README.md).
