Range & playback
Play

The notes fall to the line like in Pop Star Stage: press their button as they reach it. Same hit windows as the game (Perfect within ±80 ms, Good within ±160 ms).

Record

Press A / B / X / Y (keys J K L I) in time. When the range ends (or each time it loops) the hits are snapped and put into this level. A second pass over a looping range adds to the first. Undo takes a whole recording back.
